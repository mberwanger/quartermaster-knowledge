# Concepts

* [Commit messages say why](/knowledge/engineering/commit-messages.md) - A commit message explains why the change was made, because the diff already shows what changed. The subject is a sentence, the body is the reasoning, and the rejected alternative belongs there too.
* [Go design keeps ownership and failure explicit](/knowledge/engineering/go-best-practices.md) - High-value Go practices for interfaces, errors, context, concurrency, and tests that keep behavior understandable as a service grows.
* [Go imports group in three blocks](/knowledge/engineering/go-imports.md) - Standard library, third party, then our own modules, separated by blank lines and enforced by gci rather than by review. Configured once per repository in .golangci.yml.
* [Next.js keeps server work on the server](/knowledge/engineering/nextjs-best-practices.md) - Durable App Router practices for component boundaries, data access, caching, mutations, errors, security, performance, and testing.
